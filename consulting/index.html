Expert consulting and technical analysis

Dr. Finkelstein provides expert witness testimony and technical consulting in patent matters involving biotechnology, cell and gene therapy, molecular biology, and gene editing. His dual perspective as a working scientist at the forefront of gene editing research and a participant in foundational patent proceedings gives him a unique ability to translate complex science for legal and regulatory audiences.
